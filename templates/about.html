Our story

The hands that built this shop.

Three generations. One craft. Never outsourced.

1987

The first stitch

Uttam Tailors opens at Subhash Chowk, Sikar. One machine. One measuring tape. My grandfather laid the foundation — but it was my father who built the empire. The rule from day one: if the garment fits perfectly, the customer always comes back.

1995

Papa builds the empire

My father mastered every garment — shirts, suits, kurta, blazers, pathani. He trained tailors with patience most people do not have. Lakhs of garments delivered. Thousands of families served. Not one shortcut, ever.

2008

The shop grows

More tailors, more machines, more garments delivered across Sikar and beyond. But the same rule from 1987: every measurement taken by hand, every garment checked before delivery. My father trained each new tailor personally.

2020

Papa left us

In 2020, we lost the man who built all of this. I took over with one mission: to carry his standard forward and bring his craft to every home in India — not just Sikar.

2025

Taking it online — all of India

In 2025, Uttam Tailors goes online. Our goal: bring the quality of master tailoring to every city in India. Not just Sikar. Order from home, get measured at your door, receive your garment anywhere in the country. Papa's craft — now for all of India.
