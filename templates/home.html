The legacy

“My father spent 33 years building this craft. In 2025, I am taking it to every corner of India.”

— The family behind Uttam Tailors, Sikar

Read our full story →

In 1987, a single sewing machine and one rule: if the garment fits perfectly, the customer always comes back.

My father spent over three decades building Uttam Tailors into what Sikar knows today. Lakhs of garments. Thousands of families served. Not one shortcut taken.

In 2020 he left us, but not the craft. I took over with one mission — to carry his standard forward and bring it online, so his quality reaches not just Sikar, but all of India.

1987 — forever in every stitch
